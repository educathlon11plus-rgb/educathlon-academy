About Educathlon Academy

Building Confidence. Developing Skills. Inspiring Success.

At Educathlon Academy, we believe every child deserves the opportunity to learn with confidence, develop strong academic foundations and make meaningful progress.

Based in Berryfields, Aylesbury, we provide supportive face-to-face tuition for 11+ pupils in Years 4 and 5, KS3 Maths students and GCSE Maths students.

Our approach combines structured teaching, regular practice and personalised attention within small groups.

Our Commitment

  • Encouraging, supportive teaching
  • Structured and progressive learning
  • Small groups of up to 10 pupils
  • Regular practice and feedback
  • Confidence and independent learning

Experience That Supports Learning

We combine educational experience with a clear understanding of how children build knowledge, confidence and study habits.

15 Years of Experience

Educathlon Academy draws on 15 years of experience to provide a supportive, structured learning environment where pupils can strengthen their skills and work towards their academic goals.

We recognise that children learn at different speeds and may need different types of explanation, practice and encouragement. Our lessons aim to build understanding step by step rather than relying on memorisation alone.

Our goal is to help pupils become more confident, independent and resilient learners, with skills that extend beyond individual tests and examinations.

Our Teaching Philosophy

Strong foundations, steady progress and positive learning experiences are central to our approach.

Build Understanding

We explain concepts clearly, revisit essential skills and encourage pupils to understand the reasoning behind their answers.

Develop Confidence

We create an encouraging learning environment where pupils can ask questions, learn from mistakes and recognise their progress.

Encourage Independence

Through structured practice and feedback, pupils develop the habits and strategies needed to tackle challenges more independently.

How We Support Every Pupil

Our teaching combines group learning with attention to individual strengths and areas for development.

Small-Group Tuition

With a maximum of 10 pupils per group, we can offer more focused attention while maintaining an engaging learning environment.

Structured Weekly Lessons

Lessons follow a progressive approach, helping pupils consolidate existing knowledge before moving on to more demanding topics.

Practice and Homework

Regular classwork and homework reinforce learning, encourage independent practice and highlight areas needing further support.

Feedback and Progress

We use pupils' work and classroom progress to identify strengths, address gaps and guide future teaching.
