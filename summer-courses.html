Educathlon Academy Summer Courses

Our intensive summer courses are designed to strengthen core academic skills, prepare students for the 11+ examinations and build confidence before the new school year begins.

Course Overview

  • Advanced English – critical thinking, creative writing, comprehension, grammar, syntax, punctuation, spelling, vocabulary and a mock exam (13 days)
  • Advanced Maths – advanced tests, times tables, critical thinking, algebra, full revision programme and mock exam (7 days)
  • Verbal Reasoning & Non-Verbal Reasoning + Technical English – preparation for 11+ exam formats and mock exam (10 days)
  • Essential Maths – preparation for 11+ maths and mock exam (13 days)

Teaching material is provided for all courses and is included in the course fee. Any materials purchased before the course cannot be refunded.
