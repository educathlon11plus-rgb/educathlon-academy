More About GCSE Maths

KS3 Maths — Years 7, 8 and 9

Building strong mathematical foundations and preparing pupils for the demands of GCSE study.

Year 7 — Transition and Foundations

We support the transition from primary to secondary Maths, focusing on confidence with number, calculations, fractions, decimals and introductory algebra.

Year 8 — Developing Understanding

Pupils build on their existing skills through algebra, proportional reasoning, geometry, data handling and more demanding problem-solving.

Year 9 — Preparing for GCSE

Tuition helps pupils consolidate KS3 concepts, strengthen mathematical reasoning and prepare for the transition to GCSE-level questions.

KS3 Programme Details

Duration: One hour per week

Fee: £20 per session

Payment: Monthly in advance, based on scheduled sessions

Classwork, homework and printed learning resources are included.

GCSE Maths — Years 10 and 11

Focused tuition to develop understanding, improve accuracy and build exam confidence.

Year 10 — Building GCSE Knowledge

We help pupils strengthen their understanding of key GCSE topics, identify gaps and develop a more confident approach to multi-step problems.

Areas include number, algebra, ratio and proportion, geometry, probability and statistics.

Year 11 — Consolidation and Exam Skills

Tuition focuses on reinforcing key concepts, practising GCSE-style questions, improving problem-solving techniques and building confidence ahead of examinations.

Support is informed by each pupil's current level and learning needs.

GCSE Programme Details

Duration: One hour per week

Fee: £25 per session

Payment: Monthly in advance, based on scheduled sessions

Printed learning materials, weekly practice and feedback are included.
