Services

Tuition tailored to each stage of your child’s academic journey.

Educathlon Academy offers structured support across Primary Maths, 11+ Preparation and GCSE Maths, helping students build confidence, understanding and exam readiness.

Primary Maths

Support for number fluency, arithmetic, fractions, problem solving, measurement and reasoning, with an emphasis on confidence and core understanding.

11+ Preparation

Focused preparation across Maths, English, Verbal Reasoning and Non-Verbal Reasoning with a structured pathway for Year 3, Year 4 and Year 5 learners.

GCSE Maths

Tuition to strengthen understanding, improve problem solving and build exam confidence for secondary students preparing for GCSE Maths.

How Support Is Delivered

Small group teaching with clear explanations and guided practice.

Branded worksheets, regular checks for understanding and targeted feedback.

Structured progression that helps students move from secure foundations to exam confidence.
