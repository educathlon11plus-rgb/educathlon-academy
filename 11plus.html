Our Year 4 and Year 5 Programmes

Learning is adapted to the stage of each pupil, with skills introduced progressively and reinforced through regular practice.

FOUNDATION AND CONFIDENCE

Year 4 — Building Strong Foundations

Year 4 introduces important 11+ skills at an appropriate pace, allowing pupils to develop understanding before moving on to more demanding questions.

  • Strengthening number skills and mathematical reasoning
  • Building vocabulary, spelling and comprehension
  • Introducing verbal and non-verbal reasoning techniques
  • Developing accuracy, independence and problem-solving
  • Practising skills through structured weekly homework

APPLICATION AND EXAM READINESS

Year 5 — Developing Exam Skills

Year 5 builds on existing knowledge, helping pupils apply their skills to more challenging questions and develop effective approaches to 11+ assessments.

  • Extending mathematical problem-solving and reasoning
  • Developing comprehension, vocabulary and English accuracy
  • Practising a wider range of verbal reasoning questions
  • Strengthening non-verbal reasoning and pattern recognition
  • Improving confidence, accuracy and time management

What We Teach

Our 11+ tuition covers four core subject areas, supported by carefully prepared practice materials.

Maths

Arithmetic, fractions, decimals, percentages, geometry, word problems and mathematical reasoning.

English

Reading comprehension, vocabulary, spelling, punctuation, grammar and understanding language in context.

Verbal Reasoning

Word relationships, letter patterns, codes, sequences and logical reasoning with language.

Non-Verbal Reasoning

Shapes, patterns, rotations, reflections, sequences and visual problem-solving.

How Our Tuition Works

A consistent learning routine helps pupils build knowledge and develop good study habits.

1. Initial Assessment

We begin by understanding your child's current learning needs and areas for development.

2. Weekly Lessons

Two hours of structured face-to-face tuition each week in a small group.

3. Practice at Home

Weekly homework reinforces what pupils have learned and encourages independent practice.

4. Feedback and Progress

Feedback helps identify strengths, address gaps and guide future learning.
