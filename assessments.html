Free Initial Assessments

A simple starting point to help us understand your child's current learning needs.

1. Register Your Interest

Tell us your child's school year and the programme you're interested in.

2. Discuss Learning Needs

We'll ask about your child's strengths, confidence and any particular areas of concern.

3. Complete an Initial Assessment

Your child completes age-appropriate questions or activities to help identify their current level.

4. Receive Feedback

We'll discuss strengths, areas for improvement and suitable next steps.

11+ Mock Examinations

Helping children become familiar with examination conditions and develop greater confidence.

Why Mock Examinations Matter

Mock examinations give pupils an opportunity to practise answering questions within a set time and become more familiar with the demands of an entrance assessment.

They can help children:

  • Develop time-management skills
  • Build examination confidence
  • Practise working independently
  • Identify areas requiring further revision
  • Become familiar with exam-style questions

Experience in Conducting Mock Exams

Our experience includes conducting mock examinations for an established third-party education provider.

This practical experience informs our approach to creating a structured, supportive examination environment for pupils.

We aim to help children approach assessments with greater familiarity, preparation and confidence.
